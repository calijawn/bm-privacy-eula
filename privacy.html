Banister Financials — Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Who we are

Banister Financials is an internal application operated by Banister Tool, Inc. and Banister Medical Holdings, Inc., Texas ("Banister", "we"). It is used only by Banister staff and is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What the application accesses

With the authorization of a Banister administrator, the application connects to QuickBooks Online companies owned by Banister. It reads accounting data: chart of accounts, classes, trial balances, transaction detail, budgets, accounts receivable and payable aging, and sales by customer.

The application only reads data. It does not create, change or delete any data in QuickBooks Online.

How the data is used

The data is used for Banister's own financial reporting and analysis, including reports to Banister's lenders. Analysis may use AI services under commercial terms that do not permit the provider to train models on the data.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on Banister-controlled computers with encrypted disks.
  • QuickBooks sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's secure credential store, never in files or source code.
  • Access is limited to authorized Banister staff.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with third parties. Service providers that process the data on our behalf are bound by written terms that protect it.

Retention and disconnection

Data is kept as long as needed for Banister's financial records. A Banister administrator can disconnect the application at any time in QuickBooks Online, after which it can no longer read any data.
